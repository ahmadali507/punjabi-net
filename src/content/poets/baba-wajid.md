Baba Wajid was a Sufi poet. Scholars differ on when and where he was born: punjabi-kavita.com gives his birth as 1718?, Wikisource places him in the eighteenth century. A Pathan, he served first in the army and then joined the company of holy men and renounced the world.

His work survives in Punjabi and Hindi. According to Piara Singh Padam it comprises 2 shabads, 40 saloks, 33 tutiye, a majh, a dohra and 150 arills; fourteen further books are also attributed to him.
