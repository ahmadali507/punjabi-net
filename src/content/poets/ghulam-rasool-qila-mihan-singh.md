Maulvi Ghulam Rasool (1813–1874) was a classical Punjabi poet, scholar and orator. He was born at Kot Bhowanidas in Gujranwala district and later settled at Qila Mihan Singh, after which he is named to tell him apart from Ghulam Rasool Alampuri.

His collection *Panj Ganj* includes a *Sassi Punnun*, a siharfi and poems in praise of the Prophet.
