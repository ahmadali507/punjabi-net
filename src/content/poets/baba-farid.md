Fariduddin Ganjshakar, known as Baba Farid or Shaikh Farid, was born at Kothewal near Multan. His dates are uncertain: the Encyclopaedia of Sikhism gives 1173 to 1265 (569 to 664 AH), while other accounts place his birth in 1179 or 1188 and his death in 1266.

He studied under Qutbuddin Bakhtiar Kaki in Delhi and became his spiritual successor in the Chishti order. He settled at Ajodhan, the town now called Pakpattan, where his shrine stands.

The Guru Granth Sahib preserves four hymns and 112 shaloks under his name, and he is widely regarded as the first major poet of the Punjabi language. Whether all of them are his has been debated, but the Sikh tradition receives them as Farid's.
