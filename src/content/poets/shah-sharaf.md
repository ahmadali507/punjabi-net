Shah Sharaf (1640–1724), also called Sheikh Sharaf, came from Batala in Gurdaspur district. He was introduced to Sufism in Lahore by Sheikh Muhammad Fazil of the Qadiri Shattari order.

He wrote kafis, dohras and a *Shuturnama*. His poetry teaches that a seeker must lose the self as a seed loses itself in the plant. Bulleh Shah is the best known of the poets he influenced.
