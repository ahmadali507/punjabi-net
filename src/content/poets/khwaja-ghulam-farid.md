Khwaja Ghulam Farid (c. 1841/1845 – 24 July 1901) was a Sufi poet of the Chishti order from Bahawalpur. He died at Chacharan Sharif and was buried at Kot Mithan. His mother died when he was five and his father, Khwaja Khuda Bakhsh, when he was twelve; his brother Fakhr Jahan educated him.

Most of his work is in his mother tongue, Multani, now called Saraiki, though he also wrote in Punjabi, Urdu, Persian, Sindhi and other languages. His poems turn on death, worldly and spiritual love, and the grief that love brings.
