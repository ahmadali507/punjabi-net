Surjit Patar was born Surjit Hunjan on 14 January 1945 at Pattar Kalan in Jalandhar district, and took his pen name from the village. He studied Punjabi at Patiala, joined Punjab Agricultural University in Ludhiana in 1971, and retired there as Professor of Punjabi. He died at his home in Ludhiana on 11 May 2024.

His first collection, *Hawa Vich Likhe Harf* (1979), was a book of ghazals. *Hanere Vich Sulagdi Varnmala* (1992) won the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1993; he also received the Saraswati Samman in 2009 and the Padma Shri in 2012.

His work is in copyright, so this archive lists his books but does not reproduce his poems.
