Lala Kirpa Sagar (4 May 1875 – 19 May 1939) was born at Pipnakha in Gujranwala district, the son of Lal Maya Das. He studied to F.A., taught for a while, worked as a journalist and then as a clerk at the Punjab University, Lahore. He was also a publisher, with his own press in Ram Gali, Lahore.

His works include the epic *Lakshmi Devi*, the poems of *Man Tarang*, and the historical plays *Maharaja Ranjit Singh* (two parts) and *Dido Jamwal*.
