Miran Shah Jalandhari (1839–1914; Punjabi Wikipedia also gives 1925 as his death) was born and lived in Jalandhar. He knew Punjabi, Hindi, Urdu, Persian and Arabic, and wrote poetry in Punjabi, Hindi and Urdu.

His Punjabi works include *Heer va Ranjha*, *Mirza Sahiban*, *Sohni Mahiwal*, the *Kulliyat Miran Shah* and the *Guldasta Miran Shah*. He is best known for his kafis. A few of the kafis given here are in Hindi.
