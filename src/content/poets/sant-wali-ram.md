Sant Wali Ram (seventeenth century) was a Sufi poet who wrote in Persian, Hindi, Rekhta and Punjabi. He was closely associated with Dara Shikoh and served for twenty years at the court of the emperor Aurangzeb before leaving his post to live as a carefree mystic.

His son Nand Ram was also a spiritual poet, who joined the service of Guru Gobind Singh.
