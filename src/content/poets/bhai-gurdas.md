Bhai Gurdas (1551 – 25 August 1636) was born at Goindwal. He was a Sikh writer, historian and preacher and served as Jathedar of the Akal Takht from 1606 until his death. As Guru Arjan's amanuensis he wrote out the first version of the Guru Granth Sahib, the Adi Granth of 1604. He was a nephew of Guru Amar Das.

He knew Punjabi, Sanskrit, Braj Bhasha and Persian. His *Vaaran*, in Punjabi, are popularly called the key to the Guru Granth Sahib.
