Peelu (Pilu; c. 1580–1675 according to Wikipedia; Wikisource gives 1563–1606) was a contemporary of the emperor Akbar. punjabi-kavita.com says he came from the village of Verowal in Amritsar district.

He is known for his telling of the tragic romance of Mirza and Sahiban, and also wrote shaloks. Later poets, among them Mian Muhammad Bakhsh and Ahmad Yar, praised him, and Hafiz Barkhurdar was his disciple.
