Guru Nanak (15 April 1469 – 22 September 1539), the founder of the Sikh faith, was born at Rai Bhoi di Talwandi, now Nankana Sahib in Sheikhupura district. His parents were Mehta Kalyan Das Bedi (Mehta Kalu) and Mata Tripta; his elder sister was Bibi Nanaki.

In 1504 he went with Bibi Nanaki to Sultanpur Lodhi, where he worked for a time in the storehouse of Nawab Daulat Khan Lodhi. He made four long journeys (udasis). punjabi-kavita.com counts 947 shabads by him; his chief works include the *Japu* and *Siddh Gosht*. The texts here are from the Guru Granth Sahib.
