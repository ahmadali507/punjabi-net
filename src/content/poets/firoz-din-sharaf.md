Babu Firoz Din Sharaf (1898 – 11 March 1955) was born in Lahore, the son of Khan Veeru Khan. His sweet voice earned him the name *Punjabi Bulbul*.

He wrote on Hindu-Muslim unity, patriotism, social reform, freedom and historical figures. His books include *Dukhan de Keerne*, *Noori Darshan*, *Sunehri Kalian*, *Hijar di Agg*, *Shiromani Shaheed*, *Nabian da Sardar*, *Sharaf Hulare*, *Sharaf Udari*, *Sharaf Sunehe* and *Jogan*.
