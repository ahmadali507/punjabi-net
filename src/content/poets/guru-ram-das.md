Guru Ram Das (24 September 1534 – 1 September 1581) was born in Chuna Mandi, Lahore, and became the fourth Sikh Guru in 1574, succeeding Guru Amar Das. He founded the town of Ramdaspur, later Amritsar. His hymns, among them the *Laavan* sung at Sikh weddings, are in the Guru Granth Sahib.
