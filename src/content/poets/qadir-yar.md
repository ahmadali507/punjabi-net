Qadir Yar (1802–1892; punjabi-kavita.com gives 1803?–1892), born Qadar Baksh, was a Sandhu Jat and a poet of the Punjabi language.

He is known to most readers through his *Qissa Puran Bhagat*, told as a sequence of siharfis; Maharaja Ranjit Singh granted him a well for it. His other works include *Raja Rasalu*, *Sohni Mahiwal*, siharfis on Hari Singh Nalwa, a *Mehrajnama* and a *Rajnama*.
