Sain Maula Shah (1836–1944), also known as Sain Maula Shah Majethvi, was a Sufi poet who put many folk tales into verse: *Sassi Punnun*, *Bugha Mal Bishnu*, *Mirza Sahiban*, *Heer Ranjha*, *Zohra Mushtari* and *Chandar Badan*. He used Urdu, Punjabi, Persian and English in his writing.

In old age he lived at Tibber Sharif in Gurdaspur, growing plants and fruit trees.
