Guru Arjan (15 April 1563 – 30 May 1606) was born at Goindval and became the fifth Sikh Guru in 1581, succeeding his father, Guru Ram Das. He compiled the first version of the Sikh scripture, the *Adi Granth*, in 1604, gathering in it the hymns of the earlier Gurus and of saints such as Sheikh Farid. He was executed in Lahore in 1606 on the orders of the Mughal emperor Jahangir. His own hymns include the *Sukhmani Sahib*.
