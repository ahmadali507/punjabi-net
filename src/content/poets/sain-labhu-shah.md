Sain Labhu Shah (1880?–1947) belonged to the Naushahi branch of the Qadiri order and lived at Jagdeo Kalan, Ajnala tehsil, Amritsar district. An Arain, he was a disciple of Mian Natthe Shah, grandson of Sayyid Hashim Shah.

He wrote a qissa of Heer and Ranjha, kafis, ghazals and dohras; his dohras were published in the book *Ganj-e-Irfana*. punjabi-kavita.com records that he and his companions were swept away in the violence of 1947.
