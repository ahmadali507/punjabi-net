Sachal Sarmast (c. 1739 – 1827; punjabi-kavita.com gives 1739–1829) was born at Daraza near Ranipur in Sindh. His real name was Abdul Wahab Farouqi; "Sachal" was a nickname, from the Sindhi *sachu*, truthful.

He is called *Shair-e-Haft Zaban*, the poet of seven languages, for writing in Arabic, Sindhi, Saraiki, Punjabi, Urdu, Persian and Balochi. He is counted among the major figures of Sindhi literature. The archive gives his Punjabi and Saraiki kafis.
