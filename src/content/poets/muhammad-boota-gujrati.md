Muhammad Boota Gujrati (1843–1919, the dates given by Wikisource) was a well-known nineteenth-century Punjabi poet. By his own account he came from Gujrat, the city of Sohni.

He is best known for *Panj Ganj*, a collection of five siharfis, and for the qissa *Shirin Farhad*. Many of his lines have passed into folk proverbs.
