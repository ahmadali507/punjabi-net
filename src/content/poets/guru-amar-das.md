Guru Amar Das (5 May 1479 – 1 September 1574) was born at Basarke, near Amritsar, and became the third Sikh Guru in 1552, succeeding Guru Angad. He made Goindval his centre. His hymns, among them the *Anand Sahib*, are in the Guru Granth Sahib.
