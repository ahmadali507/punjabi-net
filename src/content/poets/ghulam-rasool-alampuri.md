Maulvi Ghulam Rasool Alampuri (29 January 1849 – 7 March 1892) belonged to the village of Alampur Kotla in Hoshiarpur district. His father, Murad Bakhsh, was a Gujjar.

His rendering of the Yusuf and Zulaikha story, *Ahsan-ul-Qasas*, written in 1873, is counted among the best Punjabi qissas. He also wrote *Dastan-e-Amir Hamza*, a siharfi on Sassi and Punnun, a baran maah of the gardener and the nightingale, and a verse letter.
