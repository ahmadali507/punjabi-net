Professor Puran Singh (17 February 1881 – 31 March 1931) was a poet, scientist and mystic, born in a Sikh family at Abbottabad. He studied industrial chemistry at Tokyo; there and after he became in turn a Buddhist monk and, under Swami Ram Tirath, a sanyasi, before settling as a Sikh mystic after meeting Bhai Vir Singh at Sialkot in 1912.

He wrote in English, Hindi and Punjabi and is one of the founders of modern Punjabi poetry. His Punjabi books include *Khulhe Maidan*, *Khulhe Ghund* and *Khulhe Asmani Rang*. The Shahmukhi text here is punjabi-kavita.com's transliteration of his Gurmukhi.
