Shah Hussain was born in 1538 in the walled city of Lahore, near the Taksali Gate, and died in 1599; one study gives 1539 to 1593 instead. His father, Sheikh Usman, was a weaver of the Dhudha clan, and Hussain sometimes called himself Faqir Hussain Julaha, Hussain the weaver. He was a student of Shaikh Bahlol Daryai of the Qadiri order.

He is also known as Madho Lal Hussain, after his devotion to Madho Lal, a Brahmin youth from Shahdara who became his disciple. The two are buried side by side at Baghbanpura, beside the Shalimar Gardens. Maharaja Ranjit Singh joined the saint's urs to the spring fair there, and the combined festival is the Mela Chiraghan, the festival of lamps.

His short kafis, built on a single refrain, set the pattern later Sufi poets followed.
