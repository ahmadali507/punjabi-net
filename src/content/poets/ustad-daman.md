Ustad Daman, born Chiragh Din in Lahore in September 1911, was a tailor by trade. He began reciting his verse at political meetings before Independence, and Jawaharlal Nehru, hearing him at a Congress gathering, called him the poet of freedom. He died in Lahore on 3 December 1984 and is buried in the courtyard of Shah Hussain's shrine at Baghbanpura.

He mocked the rulers of his day and paid for it. He was jailed under Ayub Khan, and under Z. A. Bhutto he was falsely charged with keeping explosives in what became known as the Ustad Daman bomb case. After 1947 he would not write his poems down; they were collected and published by the Ustad Daman Academy years after his death as *Daman de Moti*.

His work is in copyright, so this archive lists his books but does not reproduce his poems.
