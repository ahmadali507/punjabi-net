Mian Muhammad Bakhsh lived at Khari Sharif, near Mirpur in what is now Azad Kashmir, and is buried there. His dates are disputed: his birth is usually given as about 1830, with 1839 and 1843 also proposed, and his death as January 1907, though some accounts give 1904. His family belonged to the Qadiri Sufi tradition.

His fame rests on *Saif ul Malook*, which he also called *Safar ul Ishq*, the Journey of Love. It retells in Punjabi verse the old Arabic tale of Prince Saif ul Malook; like the rest of his work it belongs to the Sufi poetry of divine love. He finished it in 1863. He is revered across Punjab, Hazara and Azad Kashmir. He is credited with eighteen books in all, including qissas of Sohni Mahiwal, Shirin Farhad and Mirza Sahiban.
