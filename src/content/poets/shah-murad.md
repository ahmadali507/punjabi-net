Sayyid Shah Murad was a Sufi mystic and poet. Sources disagree on his dates: Wikisource, which calls him Shah Murad Khanpuri, gives 1628–1702, while punjabi-kavita.com places him in the eighteenth century. He was blind and widely known as Hafiz Murad. He was a disciple and khalifa of Sultan Nourang Shah, and his shrine is at Lunda Chand near Dera Ismail Khan.

His works include *Abyat-e-Murad*, *Charsa Murad*, *Noor Nama*, *Toba Nama*, a baramah and kafis. punjabi-kavita.com notes the Pothohari vocabulary of his baramah.
