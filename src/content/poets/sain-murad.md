Sain Murad (1882/83 – 1937/38) was a wandering faqir poet from Kot Razada in Ajnala tehsil, Amritsar district. He wrote verse letters, chaubarge, baints, *Sassi di Fagan* and a qissa of Sassi and Punnun.
