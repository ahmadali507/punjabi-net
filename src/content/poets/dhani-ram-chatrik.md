Dhani Ram Chatrik (4 October 1876 – 18 December 1954) was a poet and typographer, counted among the pioneers of modern Punjabi poetry. He standardised the typeface for the Gurmukhi script, and in 1926 became President of the Punjabi Sahit Sabha.

His language and subjects are close to everyday life. His books include *Phullan di Tokri*, *Bharthari Hari Bikramajit*, *Nal Damayanti*, *Chandanwari*, *Dharamvir*, *Kesar Kiari*, *Nawan Jahan*, *Noor Jahan Badshah Begum* and *Sufikhana*. The Shahmukhi text here is punjabi-kavita.com's transliteration of his Gurmukhi.
