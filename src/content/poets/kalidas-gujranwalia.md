Pandit Mann Singh (13 October 1865 – 1944), who signed his poems Kalidas and is known as Kalidas Gujranwalia, was born at Gujranwala to Pandit Jai Dayal, a court official and priest of Maharaja Sher Singh. Under the influence of Sant Hari Singh Gujranwalia he became a Sikh. He learned first from his father, then Urdu and Persian from the maulvi of the local mosque.

He was a leading qissa poet. His works include *Puran Bhagat*, *Gopi Chand te Raja Bharthari*, *Roop Basant*, *Raja Rasalu*, *Prahlad Bhagat*, *Raja Harish Chand*, a *Ramayan*, *Charkha Nama* and *Bhaura Kali*.
