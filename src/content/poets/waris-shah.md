Waris Shah was born around 1722 at Jandiala Sher Khan, in present-day Sheikhupura district, into a Sayyid family. He studied under Hafiz Ghulam Murtaza of Kasur and is counted a Sufi of the Chishti order. He later settled at Malka Hans, near Pakpattan, in a small room beside the mosque now named after him. He died in 1798 by most accounts, though some give 1799, and his tomb at Jandiala Sher Khan holds a yearly urs.

At Malka Hans he finished his *Heer* in 1766. It retells the love of Heer and Ranjha in thousands of lines, and its Heer has been called the greatest female character in Punjabi literature. Its popularity also drew imitators: many verses in common printed copies were added by later poets.
