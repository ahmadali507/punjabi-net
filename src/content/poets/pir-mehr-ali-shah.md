Pir Mehr Ali Shah (14 April 1859 – May 1937) was a Sufi scholar and mystic poet of the Chishti order from Punjab, in present-day Pakistan. He was a Hanafi scholar who led the movement against the Ahmadiyya.

He composed poetry in Punjabi and wrote several books in Urdu and Persian, most notably *Saif-e-Chishtiyai*.
