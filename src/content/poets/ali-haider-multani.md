Ali Haider (1690–1785), known as Ali Haider Multani, was born at Kazian in Multan district and is said to have spent most of his life in his native village. Little else is known of his life.

He wrote siharfis, a diwan and an unfinished qissa of Heer and Ranjha. punjabi-kavita.com notes that he knew many languages, carried Punjabi Sufi poetry forward, and reproached the rulers of his day for failing to stand up to Nadir Shah.
