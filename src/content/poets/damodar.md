Damodar Gulati, also known as Damodar Das Arora, lived in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, in the reign of the Mughal emperor Akbar. What little is known of him comes from his own qissa; punjabi-kavita.com places his village in Chiniot tehsil of Jhang district.

He is celebrated as the first poet to give the oral legend of Heer and Ranjha a written verse form, a tradition later poets carried on for centuries. His language is a western (Lahndi) Punjabi with Jhangi, Multani and Pothohari colour.
