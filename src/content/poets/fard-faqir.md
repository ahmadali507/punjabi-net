Fard Faqir (born 1704 or 1720, died 1790) was a Sufi poet of Gujrat district. His birth date is estimated from dates written in his works. Wikipedia quotes Baba Budh Singh calling him the saint who raised his voice against the injustice of the rulers of his time.

His works include the *Kasb-nama Bafindgan*, a *Baran Maha* and a siharfi. Another work, *Roshan Dil*, is also linked to his name, though several scholars give it to Maulvi Abdullah.
