Sultan Bahu was born at Shorkot, in present-day Jhang district, and died in 1691. His birth is usually given as 17 January 1630, though some sources say 1628. His shrine is at Garh Maharaja; it has been moved twice after the Chenab changed its course.

He belonged to the Qadiri order and is regarded as the founder of its Sarwari Qadiri branch. More than forty books on Sufism are attributed to him, nearly all in Persian.

His fame rests on his Punjabi abyat. Most of them end on the refrain hū, "He", meaning God.
