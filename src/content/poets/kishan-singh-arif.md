Kishan Singh Arif was born in Amritsar in 1836. Rekhta gives his death as 1900, the Sikh Encyclopedia as 1904. His father ran a publishing and bookselling business in Amritsar's Bazaar Mai Sewan, where he came to know qissas and books early; after his father's death he took up the same trade.

His poetry draws on Punjabi culture, romance and spiritual themes, with an emphasis on moral teaching. He also published Urdu poetry, the *Deewan-e-Arif*.
