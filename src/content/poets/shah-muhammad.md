Shah Muhammad (1780–1862) lived under Maharaja Ranjit Singh and belonged to the border village of Wadala Veeram in Amritsar district.

He is best known for his *Jangnama*, written around 1846, an account of the First Anglo-Sikh War (1845–1846) and the events after Ranjit Singh's death in 1839 that led to the downfall of the Sikh kingdom. Historians believe he drew on eyewitness accounts from relatives who served in the Sikh army.
