Muqbil (also spelled Muqbal) was a qissa poet whose full name, according to Rekhta, was Shah Jahan, Muqbil being his pen name. His retelling of Heer and Ranjha is placed in the second quarter of the eighteenth century.

Rekhta describes his *Heer* as the earliest known use of the baint in Punjabi literature.
