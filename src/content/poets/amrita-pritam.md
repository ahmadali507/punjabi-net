Amrita Pritam (born Amrit Kaur) was born in Gujranwala in 1919 and grew up in Lahore, where she lived until Partition in 1947 and published her first book of poems at sixteen. As a refugee she settled in Delhi, worked for years in the Punjabi service of All India Radio, and died there in 2005.

Her poem *Ajj Aakhaan Waris Shah Nu*, written in 1947, calls on Waris Shah to rise from his grave and record the suffering of Punjab's women during Partition. She also wrote novels, among them *Pinjar*, and an autobiography, *Rasidi Ticket*. *Sunehade* made her the first woman to receive the Sahitya Akademi Award for a Punjabi work, and *Kagaz te Canvas* brought her the Jnanpith Award.

Her work is in copyright, so this archive lists her books but does not reproduce her poems.
