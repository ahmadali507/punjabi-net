Sayyid Fazal Shah (1827–1890) was born under Sikh rule and lived at Nawan Kot, a suburb of Lahore, where he died.

He wrote qissas on tragic romances: *Sohni Mahiwal*, *Sassi Punnun*, *Heer Ranjha*, *Laila Majnun* and, by some accounts, *Yusuf Zulaikha*, as well as siharfis. *Sohni Mahiwal* is considered his best work and one of the masterpieces of the Punjabi qissa.
