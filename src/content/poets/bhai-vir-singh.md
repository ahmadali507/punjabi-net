Bhai Vir Singh was born in Amritsar in 1872 and died there in 1957. He wrote in Gurmukhi script, so any of his texts shown here in Shahmukhi are transliterations.

He worked across almost every form. He founded the Khalsa Tract Society in 1894 and the weekly *Khalsa Samachar* in 1899, wrote early Punjabi novels such as *Sundari*, and produced an annotated edition of the *Sri Gur Pratap Suraj Granth* (1927–1935). His *Rana Surat Singh*, serialised from 1905, is counted as the first Punjabi epic and the first long work in blank verse in the language.

His later short lyrics, collected in books such as *Lahiran de Har* and *Mere Saiyan Jio*, set the tone for modern Punjabi poetry. *Mere Saiyan Jio* won the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1955, and he received the Padma Bhushan in 1956.
