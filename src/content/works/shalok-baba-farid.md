The shaloks of Sheikh Farid as they stand in the Guru Granth Sahib, in that order. Of the 130 shaloks in this section, 18 are by the Sikh Gurus (4 by Guru Nanak, 5 by Guru Amar Das, 1 by Guru Ram Das, 8 by Guru Arjan), as punjabi-kavita.com notes; each is credited to its author and kept in its place, so that the numbers here are the Guru Granth Sahib's: Guru Nanak 32, 113, 120, 124; Guru Amar Das 13, 52, 104, 122, 123; Guru Ram Das 121; Guru Arjan 75, 82, 83, 105, 108, 109, 110, 111.
