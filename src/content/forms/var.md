The var is one of the oldest Punjabi forms. Its stanzas, pauris ('steps'), were sung by dhadis of battles and heroes; Najabat's var of Nadir Shah is a classic of the kind. The Sikh Gurus and Bhai Gurdas used the form for devotional poetry.
