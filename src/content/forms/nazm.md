The nazm came into Punjabi from Urdu and Persian in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. Unlike the kafi or the dohra it has no fixed shape: each poem has its own title and develops one subject. Bhai Vir Singh's short poems are among its earliest Punjabi examples.
