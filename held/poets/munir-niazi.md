Munir Niazi was born at Khanpur, a village in Hoshiarpur district. Sources disagree on the year: Wikipedia and the Daily Times give 9 April 1923, while Dawn's obituary gives 1928. After Partition his family settled in Sahiwal, where he finished school and in 1949 started a weekly paper; he later lived in Lahore and died there on 26 December 2006.

He wrote in both Urdu and Punjabi. His Punjabi collections include *Safar di Raat*, *Char Chup Cheezan* and *Rasta Dassan Wale Tare*. He received the Pride of Performance in 1992 and the Sitara-e-Imtiaz in 2005.

His work is in copyright, so this archive lists his books but does not reproduce his poems.
