Shiv Kumar Batalvi was born on 23 July 1936 (a few documents give 1937) in Bara Pind Lohtian, in Shakargarh tehsil of the old Gurdaspur district, now in Pakistan. After Partition his family moved to Batala, which gave him his pen name. He died on 6 May 1973 at Kiri Mangyal, near Pathankot, aged 36.

His lyric poems dwell on separation and grief. *Loona* (1965), a verse play that retells the Puran Bhagat legend from the side of Loona, traditionally cast as its villain, won the Sahitya Akademi Award in 1967 and made him its youngest recipient.

His work is in copyright, so this archive lists his books but does not reproduce his poems.
